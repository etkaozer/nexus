## The principle

A decision that was never written down will be re-made, badly, in about six weeks — usually by
the person who made it the first time.

## What counts as written down

The reasoning, not just the conclusion. "Spot-only for the MVP" is a fact; it is only useful
once it also says what it was protecting against, because that is what tells you whether the
condition still holds.

## Corollary

If a note has stopped being true, supersede it rather than editing it into silence. The path
matters — knowing what you used to believe, and why you stopped, is most of the value.
